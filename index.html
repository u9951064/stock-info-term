Josh Tsai Stock Info Backup

Josh Tsai Stock Info Backup is a personal, non-commercial backup tool operated by Josh Tsai. It runs on a single private server and automatically copies the operator's own Taiwan stock market research database to the operator's own Google Drive account.

What the app does

  • After each Taiwan Stock Exchange trading day, a scheduled job creates a snapshot of a local research database (publicly available market data such as daily prices and institutional trading volumes).
  • The snapshot is compressed and uploaded to a folder named stock-info-backup in the operator's Google Drive.
  • Only the five most recent daily backups are kept; older backup folders created by the app are deleted.

How it uses Google Drive

The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This lets it see, create and delete only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot read, list or modify any other file in the Google Drive account.

Who can use it

The app is used solely by its operator for their own account. It is not offered to the public, and no third-party users' data is collected.
